HyprLan is a community of hackers, designers and students in Bangalore who would rather build than scroll. We run hackathons, workshops, webinars and seminars.

Every format ends with something working, and beginners are welcome.

Questions

Who is this for?

Students and builders from any field. You do not need a finished portfolio or a computer science degree — you need something you want to make.

Do I need a team?

No. Come alone and we will help you find people, or bring two to four friends.

Online or in person?

Both. Hackathons and workshops happen in Bangalore; webinars are remote.

What should I bring?

A laptop, a charger and anything your build needs. Internet is provided at the venue.

Does it cost anything?

Most sessions are free. When an event has a fee, it is on that event's page before you register.

Can I bring a built project?

Bring the idea, not the build. Work has to be made during the event; libraries, APIs and open data are fine.
